**Option A (real data).** Public, de-identified New York State hospital discharge records. No synthetic data.

## License and terms of use

The dataset page lists its license as **"See Terms of Use."** The New York State Department of Health [Data Use Policy](https://healthweb-back.health.ny.gov/about/data_use.htm) permits reproducing the Department's published data as long as the material is kept intact and the Department is credited as the source, with the access date and the publication date. It also asks that work built on the data say the Department does not stand behind it:

> Source: New York State Department of Health, SPARCS De-Identified Hospital Inpatient Discharges, 2022 and 2023, accessed November 7, 2026. The New York State Department of Health makes no representation, warranty or guarantee relating to the data or analyses derived from these data.

**Privacy.** The public file is de-identified: no names, record numbers, exact dates or full ZIP codes. The state also suppresses fields itself. The 3-digit ZIP is blank when the ZIP's population is under 20,000, when a cell has fewer than 10 patients, or when the record is an abortion; abortion records also have the facility fields redacted. No column in this project identifies a person.

**Filter, not a sample.** Each file was exported with one filter: `Hospital Service Area = Finger Lakes`. Statewide, 2023 has about 2.1 million discharges, far over GitHub's 100 MB limit. The Finger Lakes filter keeps every discharge from the region's **17 hospitals**, so no rows were sampled. Both files have the same 33 columns in the same order.

**Coverage.** The region includes Rochester Regional Health's Rochester General, Unity, Unity Specialty, Newark-Wayne and Clifton Springs hospitals, as well as URMC (Strong, Highland, F.F. Thompson, Noyes) and others. **United Memorial (Batavia) is not in the Finger Lakes region** and is not in the data.

## From raw rows to modeling rows

| Step | 2022 (train) | 2023 (test) |
|---|---|---|
| Rows loaded | 134,596 | 137,867 |
| Remove newborn birth stays (APR MDC 15): a different discharge process | 121,823 | 125,672 |
| Remove severity code 0 (ungroupable DRGs 955/956) | 121,700 | 125,604 |
| Remove DRG × severity groups with fewer than 30 cases in 2022 | **115,840** | **118,866** |
| Extended stays (target = 1) | 23,848 | 24,331 |
| Base rate | 20.6% | 20.5% |

**Target.** `extended_stay` = 1 when Length of Stay is greater than the 75th-percentile length of stay for the same APR-DRG and APR Severity of Illness in 2022. The cutoffs come from 2022 only and are applied unchanged to 2023. 626 of 1,145 DRG × severity groups have 30 or more 2022 cases. The base rate is below 25% because whole-day stays often tie exactly at the cutoff, and ties count as not extended.

**Features (known at admission).** Facility Name, Type of Admission, Emergency Department Indicator, Age Group, Gender, Payment Typology 1, 3-digit ZIP, CCSR Diagnosis Code, APR MDC Code.

**Held out of the model (not known at admission).** APR-DRG, APR Severity of Illness, APR Risk of Mortality (assigned by coders after discharge; used only in the leakage check), CCSR Procedure, Patient Disposition, Total Charges, Total Costs and Length of Stay. Race and Ethnicity are excluded on purpose.

## Problems the file hid

None of these show up in a null check; every count comes from `model.ipynb` Step 3.

| # | Problem | 2022 | 2023 | How it was handled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Length of Stay is text**, because very long stays read `"120 +"` | 286 rows | 265 rows | `"120 +"` read as 120, then converted to a whole number |
| 2 | **Charges and costs are text with commas** (`"2,142.98"`) | 134,360 charges / 131,413 costs | 137,703 / 135,529 | Commas stripped, converted to numbers; used only for the cost-per-day estimate |
| 3 | **The same category is spelled two ways across years**: 22 of 26 MDC descriptions changed (`"AND"` became `"&"`) | — | — | Every join and group uses codes, never descriptions |
| 4 | **Severity code 0 means "missing,"** not a severity level (all DRG 955/956, blank description) | 123 rows | 68 rows | Excluded |
| 5 | **Blank 3-digit ZIP** (state suppression; see Privacy) | 1,762 rows | 1,824 rows | Recoded `"Unknown"`; `"OOS"` (out of state: 2,133 / 2,045 rows) kept as its own category |
| 6 | **Exact duplicate rows** | 70 rows | 51 rows | Kept: no patient ID exists to prove they are the same stay. 128 of 136 rows in 2022 duplicate sets are newborns, already excluded; 4 remain in training, 7 in test. See `verification.md`. |
| 7 | **IDs that are not unique**: two operating certificate numbers each cover two hospitals (Unity and Unity Specialty share one), and St. Joseph's has two | — | — | Facility Name used as the hospital feature (it matches Permanent Facility Id one-to-one) |
| 8 | **Placeholder values**: Gender `"U"`, Type of Admission `"Not Available"` | 2 / 7 rows | 4 / 12 rows | Kept as their own categories; the app does not offer them as inputs |
| 9 | **Categories only in the test year**: diagnosis codes in 2023 never seen in 2022 | — | 20 codes in the raw files; **28 codes (56 rows)** after exclusions | The encoder sends unseen and rare (<30 training rows) categories to one "infrequent" column instead of failing; the app only offers codes seen in 2022 |
| 10 | **A coding inconsistency**: 1 training row is Type of Admission `"Newborn"` but is not in the newborn body system (MDC 15) | 1 row | — | Left as is; too small to matter |

Problem 9 differs between Step 3 (20) and Step 5 (28) because Step 3 compares the raw files. After exclusions, the 2022 training set contains fewer diagnosis codes, so 8 more 2023 codes have no match.

## Known limitations of the source

- The state notes that the data is subject to change and that, when the file was released, some facilities were not in full compliance with reporting requirements ([SPARCS](https://www.health.ny.gov/statistics/sparcs/)).
- Age is only available in five groups (0–17, 18–29, 30–49, 50–69, 70 or older).
- Payer is the expected primary payer (Payment Typology 1), recorded at the time of the stay.
- The CCSR diagnosis category reflects the final principal diagnosis coded at discharge. The model uses it as a stand-in for the admitting diagnosis, an approximation stated in `model_card.md`.
