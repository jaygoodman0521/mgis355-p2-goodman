# Model card — Extended-Stay Risk at Admission

MGIS 355 Project 2 · Jay Goodman · Random forest classifier · App: see `README.md`

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Decision and user** | An inpatient case manager at a Rochester-area hospital decides, **once per admission, on the day of admission**, whether to start discharge planning immediately or leave the patient to the routine review. Early-review slots are limited, so the model ranks admissions to decide who gets one. |
| **Unit of prediction** | One row per inpatient discharge from one of the 17 hospitals in New York's Finger Lakes region. |
| **Target** | `extended_stay` = 1 if Length of Stay is greater than the 75th-percentile stay for the same APR-DRG and severity of illness, with cutoffs from 2022 applied unchanged to 2023. **Excludes** newborn birth stays (MDC 15), ungroupable stays (severity 0, DRGs 955/956), and DRG × severity groups with fewer than 30 cases in 2022. Base rate: 20.6% (2022), 20.5% (2023). |
| **Data** | Real, public: NYS DOH SPARCS De-Identified Hospital Inpatient Discharges, discharge years 2022 and 2023, Finger Lakes region. 134,596 + 137,867 rows loaded; 115,840 + 118,866 after exclusions. Details in `data.md`. |
| **Features** | Hospital (Facility Name) — known at prediction time? **yes** · Type of Admission — **yes** · Came through the ED — **yes** · Age Group — **yes** · Gender — **yes** · Primary payer (Payment Typology 1) — **yes**, at registration · 3-digit ZIP — **yes** · CCSR Diagnosis category — **approximately**: stands in for the admitting diagnosis; the final principal diagnosis is coded at discharge · APR MDC (body system) — **approximately**, derived from the diagnosis. |
| **Excluded on purpose** | **APR-DRG, Severity of Illness, Risk of Mortality, Medical/Surgical flag**: assigned by coders after discharge (leakage). Adding the first three lifts test AUC only from 0.639 to 0.657, because the target is already defined within DRG × severity. **CCSR Procedure, Patient Disposition, Total Charges, Total Costs, Length of Stay**: only known during or after the stay. **Race, Ethnicity**: deliberately not used to rank patients. **Operating Certificate Number**: not a unique hospital ID. **Permanent Facility Id**: duplicates Facility Name. **Payment Typology 2–3**: secondary payers are often settled after admission. **Birth Weight**: only meaningful for the excluded newborns. **Hospital Service Area, Hospital County, Discharge Year**: constant within the filtered data, or they define the split. |
| **Split** | **Time split.** Train on 2022 (115,840 rows), test once on 2023 (118,866 rows). The model will be used on admissions that have not happened yet, so it is judged on a year it never saw. Settings were chosen with cross-validation inside 2022 only. |
| **Baseline** | (1) Always "not extended": **79.5% accuracy, 0 of 24,331 extended stays caught.** (2) The better simple rule, each diagnosis category's 2022 extended-stay rate: **AUC 0.571.** Flagging the same 30.9% of admissions as the model, it catches **8,981** extended stays. |
| **Model** | Random forest (200 trees, min_samples_leaf = 10, chosen by 3-fold CV on 2022) on one-hot features. **Test AUC 0.639** against 0.571 for the rule and 0.628 for logistic regression. At the chosen threshold it flags 30.9% of 2023 admissions and **catches 10,843 of 24,331 extended stays (44.6%)**, with 29.5% of flags correct (1.44× the base rate). That is **1,862 more stays and about $3.1M more net savings than the rule at the same volume.** Its edge over logistic regression on 2023 is small: 120 stays and $0.2M. The forest was chosen on 2022 out-of-fold results, before the test year was scored. |
| **Threshold and cost** | **Miss** (extended stay not flagged): 0.73 bed-days not saved × **$2,272** per day (median Total Costs ÷ Length of Stay, 2022 SPARCS) = **$1,659**. The 0.73 days is the Cochrane discharge-planning estimate for older adults admitted without surgery ([AAFP summary, 2022](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2022/1100/cochrane-discharge-plans)), applied here to all adults, which is an assumption. **Each review**: 0.5 hours (assumption) × $45.42/hour, the BLS national mean wage for registered nurses, May 2023 ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/May/oes291141.htm)) = **$22.71**. At face value a miss costs 73× a review, which implies a threshold of 0.014: review everyone, which no team can staff. I treated **review capacity as the binding constraint** (30% of admissions), which implies a **threshold of 0.244**, set on 2022 out-of-fold predictions. Estimated net savings on 2023: **$17.15M** across the region, an estimate under these assumptions, not a result. |
| **Where it is weakest** | **Patients 70 and older: AUC 0.600** (vs 0.639 overall) on 45,456 test stays. Medicare patients, mostly the same people, also score 0.600. The model flags 55% of older patients largely for their age and cannot tell which of them will run long. Mental health admissions run long 24.2% of the time, but only 36.8% of those are caught. |
| **Do not use it for** | **Comparing hospital performance.** Hospital is the strongest feature, and 2022 rates range from 9.5% to 46.6%, but the data cannot separate case mix within a DRG, post-acute bed availability or discharge practice. Also not for **decisions about an individual patient's care** (it orders a review queue; it does not replace a case manager's assessment), or for **hospitals outside the region or years far from 2022–2023** without re-testing. |
| **What would change the recommendation** | If, on a new year, the forest flagging the same volume no longer catches more extended stays than the diagnosis rule (2023: **10,843 vs 8,981**), use the simpler rule. If a pilot measures early planning saving under **0.034 days** per caught stay, the reviews cost more than they save at this threshold (break-even: 36,723 reviews × $22.71 ÷ (10,843 caught × $2,272)). If review capacity is far below 30%, the list shrinks but gets sharper: at 10% of admissions, flags were right 34% of the time (1.66× base rate) but caught only 17% of extended stays (2022 out-of-fold). |
